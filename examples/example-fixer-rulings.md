# Example Fixer Rulings

## Question: Does Suspicious mean Fake?

No. Suspicious means the document may work depending on Heat and clearance.

## Question: Can a Deported player talk?

Yes. Deported players can still talk unless the script says otherwise.

## Question: Does a blocked player know they were blocked?

Usually they receive no result. They may infer they were blocked, but The Fixer should not confirm unless the role says so.

## Question: Can a player lie about their role?

Yes. Lying is allowed.
