# Example First Round

## Setup

Alice is Embassy Contact. Clara is a Nomad with an Expired Visa Stamp. Hugo is Fake Visa Agent.

## Night 1

Alice checks Clara's Visa Stamp.

The Fixer says:

> Invalid.

Hugo gives Emma a Fake Proof of Funds.

## Morning Briefing

Crisis: Random Document Check.

The group must choose one player for a Soft Audit.

## Private Chats

Alice tells Clara:

> I think your Visa Stamp has a problem.

Clara denies it honestly because she does not know it is expired.

## Public Accusation

Alice pushes for a Soft Audit on Clara.

Hugo tries to redirect the audit toward Emma.

## Vote

The group audits Clara.

## Crisis Reveal

The Fixer says:

> Clara's Dossier contains at least one problematic document. Clara becomes Flagged. Heat increases by 1.
