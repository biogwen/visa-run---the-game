# Example Dossier Audit

## Situation

The group suspects Maya because two players who talked to her received bad documents.

## Vote

The group chooses a Document Audit on Maya's Local Contact.

## Fixer result

The Fixer says:

> Maya's Local Contact is Suspicious.

## Consequence

Maya becomes Flagged, but her alignment is not revealed.

## Interpretation

The group should ask whether Maya created the problem or received it from someone else.
