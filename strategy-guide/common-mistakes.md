# Common Mistakes

## Mistake 1: Treating every liar as evil

Good players often lie because their Dossier is fragile.

## Mistake 2: Ignoring document chains

Do not stop at who owns the fake document. Ask where it came from.

## Mistake 3: Auditing everything

Audits create clarity but may increase Heat.

## Mistake 4: Revealing information too early

Information roles become targets.

## Mistake 5: Forgetting Independents

Not all chaos comes from Scammers.

## Mistake 6: Letting Heat become invisible

Heat should change decisions every day.
