# How Nomads Detect Scammers

Nomads should not only ask: who lied?

They should ask: who made Dossiers harder to read?

## Look for patterns

- Same player connected to multiple fake documents
- Same player blocks or attacks information roles
- Same player pushes fast solutions
- Same player benefits when Heat rises
- Same player changes story after audits

## Strong deduction method

Track four things:

1. Information received
2. Source of information
3. Reliability of source
4. Players connected to the result

A fake document does not prove its owner is evil. Ask where it came from.
