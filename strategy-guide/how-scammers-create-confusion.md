# How Scammers Create Confusion

Scammers win by making good players dangerous to each other.

## Strong Scammer tactics

- Give real help early
- Plant fake documents later
- Make victims defend fake documents honestly
- Block checks at key moments
- Push audits onto good players with Hidden Complications
- Raise Heat through group decisions

## Bad Scammer tactics

- Always lie
- Always defend other Scammers
- Make every problem trace back to you
- Increase Heat too obviously
