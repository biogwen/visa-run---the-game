# Private Chat Strategy

Private Chats are where trust is built and destroyed.

## Nomad tips

- Share partial information first
- Ask testable questions
- Compare timelines
- Do not reveal everything to one person
- Ask who gave which document

## Scammer tips

- Offer help before being asked
- Create dependency
- Give one true detail with one false conclusion
- Make private deals that are hard to verify

## Independent tips

- Find desperate players
- Offer personal solutions
- Stay useful but not too central
