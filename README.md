# Visa Run Wiki

Welcome to the official working wiki for **Visa Run: The Border Is Closing**.

Visa Run is a social deduction game about digital nomads trapped in a country where the border is about to close. The Nomads need enough valid documents to leave. The Scammers want to sabotage the group with fake papers, blocked checks, bad advice and Heat. Independent players have selfish objectives that may or may not help the group.

The central question is not only: **Who is evil?**

The real question is: **Who can actually leave the country, who is lying about their paperwork, and whose advice is going to ruin us?**

## Design principle

**Everyone needs the group to escape, but almost nobody can be fully honest about their situation.**
