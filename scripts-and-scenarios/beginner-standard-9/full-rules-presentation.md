# Full Rules Presentation

Welcome to **Visa Run: The Border Is Closing**.

You are digital nomads trying to leave the country before the border closes.

Most of you are Nomads. Some of you are Scammers. One of you may have a personal objective.

Each of you has a Dossier. Having a document does not mean it is valid.

Each day, the group may send one player to the Embassy or Immigration Office.

Embassy is safer.

Immigration is stricter and more dangerous.

Heat makes the border stricter.

If you are Deported, you can still talk, but you do not count as a legal exit.

Nomads need 5 legal exits. Scammers win if 4 or fewer players can leave.

You may lie, bluff, share information or hide your problems.

The real question is:

> Who is making the Dossiers impossible to trust?
