# Coworking Variant

Coworking Conspiracy is a thematic variant.

## Theme

The group is in a coworking space. Someone is sabotaging Wi-Fi, leads, calls and trust.

## Replace documents with resources

- Wi-Fi Access
- Client Lead
- Desk Booking
- Meeting Room
- Payment Link
- Laptop Charger

## Replace Heat with Reputation

High Reputation damage makes clients leave and resources fail.
