# Public Accusation

Public Accusation is the main group discussion.

## Players should discuss

- Who is connected to fake documents
- Who tried to stop useful audits
- Who keeps increasing Heat
- Who has inconsistent stories
- Which Dossiers need help
- Which crisis choice is safest

## Fixer role

The Fixer should not solve debates. The Fixer only clarifies rules, timing and public game state.

## Good accusation

A good accusation is not just: "I think Alex is evil."

A stronger accusation is: "Two fake documents appeared after Alex offered help, and Maya blocked both attempts to verify them."
