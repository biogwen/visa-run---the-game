# Private Chats

Private Chats are short private conversations between players.

## Recommended timing

- 5 minutes for 7 to 9 players
- 7 minutes for 10 to 12 players
- 8 to 10 minutes for 13 to 15 players

## What players can do

Players may:

- Share information
- Lie
- Show documents if the script allows it
- Ask for help
- Make deals
- Coordinate votes
- Test each other

## Important rule

Private Chats should be long enough to create paranoia, but short enough that the public phase still matters.
