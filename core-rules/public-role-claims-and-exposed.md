# Public Role Claims and Exposed

Exact public role claims create risk.

If a player publicly claims their exact role name, mark them **Exposed**.

Example exact claim:

> I am the Embassy Contact.

Example vague claim:

> I can check documents.

Vague claims do not make a player Exposed.

Recommended beginner rule:

> If 4 or more players are Exposed at the same time, Heat +1.
