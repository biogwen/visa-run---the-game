# Morning Briefing

Morning Briefing is when The Fixer reveals the Crisis Card for the day.

## Purpose

The crisis gives the group a shared problem. It should create pressure without solving the game.

## Example briefing

> Day 2. The immigration website is down. No online validations can happen today. The group may send one player to the embassy or use a private agent.

## Fixer rule

Read the public text clearly. Keep private resolution rules hidden unless the card says they are public.
