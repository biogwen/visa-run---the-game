# Crisis Reveal

Crisis Reveal is when The Fixer resolves the public decision and hidden consequences.

## The reveal should do three things

1. Confirm the immediate result
2. Apply Heat or status changes
3. Leave enough uncertainty for the next day

## Example

The group audits Clara.

The Fixer says:

> Clara's Dossier contains one problematic document. Clara becomes Flagged. Heat increases by 1.

The Fixer does not reveal every detail unless the audit type allows it.
