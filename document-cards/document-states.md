# Document States

A document can have one of several states.

## Valid

Works at the border.

## Fake

Fails if checked.

## Expired

Needs repair or update.

## Suspicious

May work depending on Heat.

## Stolen

Belongs to someone else and raises Heat if exposed.

## Tampered

Was changed by sabotage.

## Pending

Will resolve later.

## Unknown

The true state is not yet revealed.
