# Fake Documents

Fake Documents look useful but fail when checked.

## How they appear

- Fake Visa Agent creates them
- Crisis Cards introduce them
- Bad Advice converts risky documents into fake ones
- Scammer effects plant them in Dossiers

## When exposed

When a Fake Document is exposed, usually:

- Destroy the document
- Increase Heat by 1
- Mark the player as Flagged if appropriate
