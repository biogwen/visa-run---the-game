# How Documents Are Destroyed

Documents can be destroyed, removed or invalidated.

## Causes

- Failed audit
- Crisis consequence
- Scammer sabotage
- High Heat threshold
- Player choice
- Final Border Check failure

## Destroyed versus invalidated

Destroyed means the card is removed.

Invalidated means the card remains in the Dossier but no longer helps.

## Fixer advice

Destroy documents when the game needs clarity. Invalidate documents when the game needs tension.
