# Document Card Overview

Document Cards are the material foundation of Visa Run.

They represent passports, visa stamps, tickets, proof of funds and other paperwork.

## Two truths

Every document has:

1. A visible identity, which the player may see.
2. A hidden validity, which The Fixer may know.

## Main document types

- Core Documents
- Supporting Documents
- Risk Documents
- Fake Documents
- Special Documents

## Design purpose

Document Cards create uncertainty without needing constant role reveals.
