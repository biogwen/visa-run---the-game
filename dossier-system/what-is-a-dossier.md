# What is a Dossier?

A **Dossier** is a player's immigration file.

It contains their identity, documents, hidden complications and status tokens. It determines whether they can legally leave during the Final Border Check.

## The central rule

A player may know what documents they have, but not whether those documents are truly valid.

A player can honestly say:

> I have an Exit Ticket.

But that does not mean:

> My Exit Ticket is valid.

## Why it matters

The Dossier system makes good players look suspicious. It also lets Scammers create problems that victims may defend honestly.

This turns the game from simple role hunting into social investigation.
