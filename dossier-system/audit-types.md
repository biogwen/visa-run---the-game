# Audit Types

Audits are how the group turns suspicion into information.

Audits should never be free. They create clarity, but they may increase Heat.

## Soft Audit

The Fixer gives vague information.

Example:

> This Dossier has at least one problem.

Low risk. Low clarity.

## Document Audit

The group checks one document type on one player.

Example:

> Check Maya's Exit Ticket.

The Fixer answers with a limited result such as Valid, Invalid, Suspicious or Cannot Confirm.

## Full Dossier Audit

The group checks the whole Dossier.

The Fixer gives an Exit Readiness rating:

- Green
- Yellow
- Orange
- Red
- Black

High clarity. High risk.

## Random Border Audit

Triggered by crises or high Heat. The Fixer checks a random document or hidden issue.

This is dangerous because the player cannot control what gets exposed.
