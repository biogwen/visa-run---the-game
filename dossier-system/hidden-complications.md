# Hidden Complications

Hidden Complications are secret problems inside a Dossier.

They are essential because they make good players act suspicious.

## Examples

### Overstay Risk

If publicly revealed, Heat increases by 2.

### Wrong Name

Your Passport or Exit Ticket has a name mismatch. You need an Embassy Letter to leave.

### No Proof of Funds

You need a Supporting Document before the Final Border Check.

### Remote Work Exposure

If Remote Work Crackdown appears, you become Flagged.

### Expired Visa

Your Visa Stamp starts Expired. It must be repaired or cleared.

### Police Warning

If you are audited twice, you are Deported.

## Design purpose

Hidden Complications make the table ask: is this player evil, or just in trouble?
