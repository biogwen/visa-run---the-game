# Supporting Documents

Supporting Documents strengthen a Dossier.

## Examples

- Proof of Funds
- Travel Insurance
- Local Address
- Embassy Letter
- Tax Certificate
- Police Clearance
- Work Contract
- Hotel Booking
- Bank Statement
- Local Contact

## When they matter

Supporting Documents matter more as Heat rises.

At low Heat, players may leave with only core documents.

At Heat 2 or higher, most scripts should require at least one Supporting Document.

At Heat 4 or higher, players may need two Supporting Documents or a Cleared core document.
