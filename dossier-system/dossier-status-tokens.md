# Dossier Status Tokens

Status Tokens mark the current state of a player or their Dossier.

## Active

The player can talk, vote, act and count as a legal exit.

## Flagged

The authorities noticed the player.

Effect: the player needs one extra Supporting Document at the border.

## Cleared

One part of the Dossier has been verified.

Effect: may help suspicious documents pass or reduce Heat, depending on script.

## Compromised

The Dossier has been touched by sabotage.

Effect: the next audit may return incomplete information, or one document may have been changed.

## Protected

The Dossier cannot be changed tonight.

## Under Review

A document is Pending and will resolve later.

## Deported

The player no longer counts as a legal exit, but can still talk and may still win with their team.
