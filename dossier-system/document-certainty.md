# Document Certainty

Document Certainty is the gap between what a player sees and what The Fixer knows.

## Visible document

The player may see:

> Exit Ticket to Bangkok, Friday 22:40.

## Hidden validity

The Fixer may know:

- Valid
- Fake
- Expired
- Suspicious
- Stolen
- Tampered
- Wrong Name
- Pending
- Unknown

## Core design effect

A player can defend a document honestly and still be wrong.

This lets Scammers plant documents that good players will protect without realizing they are fake.
