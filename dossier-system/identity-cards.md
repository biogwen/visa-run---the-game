# Identity Cards

Identity Cards define how a player appears in the immigration system.

## Purpose

Identity gives crises and document checks something to target.

A Remote Worker may be affected by Remote Work Crackdown. A Crypto Nomad may be vulnerable to Proof of Funds checks. A Backpacker may be less suspicious but less prepared.

## Example identities

- Tourist
- Remote Worker
- Freelancer
- Founder
- Backpacker
- Consultant
- Crypto Nomad
- Student
- Content Creator

## Public or hidden?

For the first prototype, Identity should be private but revealable through some audits and crises.

For a lighter game, make Identity public.
