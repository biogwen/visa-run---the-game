# Glossary

## Active

Can count as legal exit.

## Black

Serious Dossier issue.

## Cleared

Verified document or player.

## Deported

Can talk but does not count as legal exit.

## Embassy Check

Safer Full Dossier Check.

## Exposed

Exact public role claim made.

## Immigration Office Check

Stricter, riskier Full Dossier Check.

## Network Hint

Partial Scammer information.

## Official Check

Daily Full Dossier Check.

## Shortcut Token

Temporary risky workaround.

## Unofficial Document

Useful-looking document that must be Cleared before safe use.
