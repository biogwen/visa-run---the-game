# Glossary

## Active

A player who can fully participate, vote and count as a legal exit.

## Audited

A player or document has been checked by an official process.

## Cleared

A document or player has been partially verified.

## Compromised

A Dossier has been touched by sabotage.

## Core Document

A main document required to leave, such as Passport, Visa Stamp or Exit Ticket.

## Crisis Card

A public event revealed during Morning Briefing.

## Deported

A player removed from official border processing. They can still talk but do not count as a legal exit.

## Dossier

A player's immigration file.

## Fake

A document state that fails when checked.

## Flagged

A player is under official suspicion.

## Heat

The global pressure level. Higher Heat means stricter border checks.

## Hidden Complication

A private weakness inside a Dossier.

## Scammer

An evil team player trying to stop enough legal exits.

## Suspicious

A document state that may pass at low Heat but may fail at high Heat.

## The Fixer

The Game Master.

## Valid Exit

A player successfully passes the Final Border Check.
