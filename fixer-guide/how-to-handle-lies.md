# How to Handle Lies

Players are allowed to lie.

The Fixer should not correct player lies unless they are lying about the rules in a way that breaks the game.

## Allowed lies

- Role claims
- Document claims
- Private chat claims
- Alignment claims
- Claims about received information

## Not allowed

- Misquoting The Fixer as a rules authority
- Inventing illegal actions
- Physically altering components without permission

## Fixer phrase

Use:

> I cannot confirm that. Players may say what they want.
