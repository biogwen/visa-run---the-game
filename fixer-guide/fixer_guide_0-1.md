# Fixer Guide v0.1

## Visa Run: The Border Is Closing

This guide is for **The Fixer**, the Game Master of Visa Run.

The Fixer runs the game, manages secret information, resolves documents, controls the Crisis Cards, tracks Heat, and decides who can legally leave during the Final Border Check.

The Fixer is not trying to beat the players.

The Fixer’s job is to create tension, keep the game fair, and make sure the story stays clear enough to follow but messy enough to be fun.

---

# 1. What Players Need to Know

Read this page to all players before the game starts.

## Quick Player Briefing

You are digital nomads trapped in a country where the border is about to close.

Your goal is simple:

**Leave before the border closes.**

But there is a problem.

Your paperwork may not be as clean as you think.

Some players are trying to help the group escape.

Some players are secretly trying to sabotage the group.

Some players have their own personal objectives.

## The Basics

You are trying to leave before the border closes.

Most players are **Nomads**.

Some players are **Scammers**.

Some players have **personal objectives**.

Your **Dossier** may contain fake, expired or suspicious documents.

You may not know your own documents are bad.

Talk privately, share information, negotiate, accuse, bluff, or lie if needed.

The group votes each day.

**Heat** makes the border stricter.

Deported players still talk, but they do not count as legal exits.

At the end, The Fixer checks who can legally leave.

## The Most Important Rule

Having a document does not mean the document is valid.

You may honestly say:

> “I have an Exit Ticket.”

But that does not mean:

> “My Exit Ticket will pass the border check.”

Some documents are Valid.

Some are Fake.

Some are Expired.

Some are Suspicious.

Some may become worse as Heat rises.

## Your Goal

If you are a **Nomad**, you win if enough players legally leave the country.

If you are a **Scammer**, you win if too few players legally leave.

If you are **Independent**, check your personal win condition.

## How to Play Well

Talk to people.

Ask questions.

Compare stories.

Pay attention to who gives documents, who blocks audits, who pushes risky solutions, and who benefits from confusion.

Do not assume that a suspicious player is evil.

Good players can have bad paperwork.

Bad players can give useful help.

The question is not only:

> “Who is lying?”

The better question is:

> “Who is making the Dossiers harder to understand?”

# 2. Fixer Responsibilities

As The Fixer, you are responsible for:

- Setting up roles
- Building player Dossiers
- Managing secret document states
- Running the Night Phase
- Revealing Crisis Cards
- Timing private chats
- Managing votes
- Updating the Heat Track
- Resolving audits and document checks
- Running the Final Border Check
- Explaining rules without giving away secret information

You should stay neutral.

Do not help the Nomads too much.

Do not help the Scammers too much.

Your job is to make sure both sides have a real chance.

# 5. Core Concepts for The Fixer

## Dossier

Each player has a Dossier.

A Dossier represents that player’s immigration situation.

It may contain:

- Identity
- Core Documents
- Supporting Documents
- Hidden Complication
- Status Tokens

## Document Certainty

Players may know what documents they have, but not whether those documents are actually valid.

The Fixer knows the true state of important documents.

Example:

A player may have a visible card called:

> Exit Ticket

But secretly, The Fixer may know it is:

> Fake Exit Ticket

or:

> Suspicious Exit Ticket

or:

> Wrong Name Exit Ticket

## Heat

Heat represents official attention.

The higher the Heat, the stricter the border becomes.

Heat starts at **0**.

Heat can rise when:

- fake documents are exposed
- audits reveal major problems
- the group follows bad advice
- Scammers trigger certain effects
- crises are failed
- Overstay Risk is exposed

Heat can decrease when:

- documents are officially cleared
- the group solves a crisis cleanly
- the group Deports a confirmed Scammer
- a Local Fixer cancels the right crisis

---

# 6. Heat Track v0.1

Use this simple Heat Track for the first playtest.

| Heat | Effect |
|---:|---|
| 0 | Border checks are light. |
| 1 | Basic paperwork is checked. |
| 2 | Players need one Supporting Document. |
| 3 | Suspicious documents become risky. |
| 4 | Players need two Supporting Documents or one Cleared document. |
| 5 | Before exit, each Active player has one random document checked. |
| 6 | Border Lockdown. Scammers win unless a special effect prevents it. |

## Fixer Advice

Do not raise Heat too fast in the first game.

For v0.1, Heat should usually reach **3 or 4** by the final day.

If Heat reaches 6 too early, the game may feel unfair.

Each player starts with:

- 1 Role card
- 1 Dossier
- 3 Core or Supporting Documents
- 0 or 1 Hidden Complication

## Core Documents

Use these:

- Passport
- Visa Stamp
- Exit Ticket
- Entry Proof

## Supporting Documents

Use these:

- Proof of Funds
- Travel Insurance
- Local Address
- Embassy Letter
- Bank Statement
- Local Contact
- Police Clearance

## Hidden Complications

Use a few of these:

- Overstay Risk
- Expired Visa
- Wrong Name
- Missing Exit Ticket
- Fake Proof of Funds
- Remote Work Exposure

## Important Setup Principle

Do not make every Dossier broken.

A good v0.1 setup should include:

- 2 players with mostly clean Dossiers
- 3 players with minor issues
- 2 players with serious hidden problems
- 2 players whose Dossiers become dangerous because of Scammer actions

The game is more interesting when some players are genuinely fine and others only think they are fine.

# 9. Game Flow

Each day follows this structure:

1. Night Phase
2. Morning Briefing
3. Private Chats
4. Public Accusation
5. Vote Phase
6. Crisis Reveal

The game lasts up to **5 days**.

After Day 5, run the **Final Border Check**.

---

# 10. Night Phase

During the Night Phase, The Fixer wakes or privately handles roles in order.

## Night Order v0.1

Use this order:

1. Corrupt Officer
2. Fake Visa Agent
3. Local Fixer
4. Embassy Contact
5. Paranoid Planner
6. Fixer cleanup

## 1. Corrupt Officer

The Corrupt Officer chooses one player.

That player’s ability or official check is blocked tonight.

Suggested Fixer phrase:

> “Choose one player to block tonight.”

Record the blocked player secretly.

## 2. Fake Visa Agent

The Fake Visa Agent chooses one player.

They may add one Fake or Suspicious document to that player’s Dossier.

Suggested Fixer phrase:

> “Choose one player to receive a fake or suspicious document.”

Do not tell the target player the document is fake.

If the document is physically added, add it face down or as a normal-looking document.

## 3. Local Fixer

If the Local Fixer uses their once-per-game ability, they may repair one document or cancel one Crisis effect.

Suggested Fixer phrase:

> “Do you want to use your ability tonight?”

If blocked by the Corrupt Officer, the ability fails.

Do not reveal why it failed unless the rules require it.

## 4. Embassy Contact

The Embassy Contact chooses one player and one document type.

They learn whether that document is:

- Valid
- Invalid
- Unknown

Suggested Fixer phrase:

> “Choose one player and one document type.”

Examples:

- “Ben’s Exit Ticket”
- “Emma’s Proof of Funds”
- “Diego’s Visa Stamp”

If blocked, say:

> “No result.”

Do not explain why.

## 5. Paranoid Planner

The Paranoid Planner receives a hint about tomorrow’s Crisis.

Suggested Fixer phrase:

> “Tomorrow’s crisis is connected to Exit Tickets.”

Keep the hint useful, but not too clear.

Good hints:

- “Tomorrow’s crisis affects Exit Tickets.”
- “Tomorrow’s crisis punishes Flagged players.”
- “Tomorrow’s crisis may increase Heat.”
- “Tomorrow’s crisis involves the embassy.”

Bad hints:

- “Tomorrow’s card is Flight Cancelled.”
- “You should audit Hugo.”
- “Maya is dangerous.”

## 6. Fixer Cleanup

After all night actions:

- Update notes
- Mark blocked abilities
- Add fake documents
- Prepare the next Crisis Card
- Check Heat effects
- Prepare the Morning Briefing

---

# 11. Morning Briefing

At the start of each day, reveal one Crisis Card.

Read the public text clearly.

Do not explain hidden consequences unless the card says so.

Example:

> “Day 2. Immigration Website Down. No online validation can happen today. The group may use a private agent, but Heat may rise.”

The Morning Briefing should create pressure.

Players should immediately feel:

> “We need to decide what to do today.”

---

# 12. Private Chats

After the Morning Briefing, give players private discussion time.

Recommended timing:

| Player Count | Private Chat Time |
|---:|---:|
| 7 to 9 | 5 minutes |
| 10 to 12 | 6 minutes |
| 13 to 15 | 8 minutes |

For v0.1 with 9 players:

**5 minutes is enough.**

## What Players Can Do

During private chats, players may:

- share role claims
- show documents if your rules allow it
- lie about documents
- ask for help
- build alliances
- accuse quietly
- plan votes
- make deals

## Fixer Job During Private Chats

The Fixer should:

- watch the time
- answer rules questions quietly
- avoid giving strategy advice
- prevent players from leaving the play area too far
- make sure everyone gets a chance to talk

Do not listen too aggressively unless a role requires it.

The game should feel private.

---

# 13. Public Accusation

After private chats, bring everyone back.

Players discuss openly.

Recommended time:

**5 to 10 minutes**

The Fixer should allow strong debate, but keep it moving.

## Useful Prompts

If players are stuck, ask:

> “Who do you want to audit today?”

or:

> “What problem does the group need to solve before the vote?”

or:

> “Does anyone want to make a public accusation?”

Do not ask:

> “Who do you think is evil?”

That makes the game too close to Werewolf.

Better prompts are about Dossiers and documents.

---

# 14. Vote Phase

Each day, the group votes on one major action.

## Available Vote Actions v0.1

Use these for the first version:

### 1. Audit a Document

The group chooses one player and one document type.

The Fixer reveals whether that document is:

- Valid
- Invalid
- Suspicious
- Unknown

### 2. Soft Audit a Player

The Fixer gives vague information about a player’s Dossier.

Possible answers:

- “This Dossier appears clean.”
- “This Dossier contains at least one problem.”
- “This Dossier is difficult to verify.”
- “This Dossier has been interfered with.”

### 3. Deport a Player

That player becomes Deported.

A Deported player:

- can still talk
- can still win with their team
- has one final vote for the rest of the game
- does not count as a legal exit

### 4. Clear a Player

If the chosen player has at least one valid key document, mark one document as Cleared.

This can help during the Final Border Check.

### 5. Follow a Crisis Option

Some Crisis Cards offer a specific choice.

Example:

- Use a private agent
- Send someone to the embassy
- Pay a border fee
- Ignore the crisis
- Validate one document at the cost of Heat

## Voting Method

Use simple majority.

If tied, The Fixer may choose one of these methods before the game starts:

- no action happens
- the most recently Flagged player breaks the tie
- Heat increases by 1 and the vote is repeated
- The Fixer resolves the least aggressive option

For v0.1, use:

**If tied, no action happens and Heat increases by 1.**

This makes indecision dangerous.

---

# 15. Crisis Reveal

After the vote, resolve the Crisis Card.

The Crisis Reveal should show consequences, but not reveal everything.

Example:

> “The embassy confirms one document in Clara’s Dossier is invalid. They do not say which one.”

Good Crisis Reveals create new questions.

Bad Crisis Reveals solve the game too quickly.

## Fixer Rule

Reveal consequences, not full truth.

Say:

> “One document is invalid.”

Do not say:

> “Her Visa Stamp is expired and she is definitely good.”

Unless the card or ability specifically allows that.

---

# 16. Audits

Audits are the main official information tool.

They should be useful, but not free.

## Document Audit

A Document Audit checks one document.

Example:

> “Check Ben’s Exit Ticket.”

Possible Fixer answers:

- Valid
- Invalid
- Suspicious
- Unknown

Use **Invalid** for:

- Fake
- Expired
- Stolen
- Wrong Name
- Tampered beyond use

Use **Suspicious** for documents that may work at low Heat but fail at high Heat.

Use **Unknown** when:

- the document is hidden
- the document is pending
- a role effect blocks clear information
- the card allows uncertainty

## Soft Audit

A Soft Audit checks the general condition of a Dossier.

Possible answers:

- “This Dossier appears clean.”
- “This Dossier contains at least one problem.”
- “This Dossier contains a serious issue.”
- “This Dossier has been recently changed.”
- “This Dossier cannot be fully verified.”

Soft Audits are useful early.

They should not fully solve the game.

## Full Dossier Audit

Do not use Full Dossier Audits often in v0.1.

They are powerful and can create too much clarity.

If used, give one of these results:

- Green: likely to pass
- Yellow: passable if Heat stays low
- Orange: needs help
- Red: cannot pass without repair
- Black: dangerous, may increase Heat if exposed

A Full Dossier Audit may increase Heat by 1 or 2 if it reveals fake documents, Overstay Risk, or serious fraud.

---

# 17. Document States

Use these states in v0.1.

## Valid

Works during the Final Border Check.

## Fake

Fails if checked.

If publicly exposed, Heat increases by 1.

## Expired

Fails unless repaired or Cleared.

## Suspicious

May pass at low Heat.

At Heat 4 or higher, Suspicious documents fail unless Cleared.

## Pending

Not resolved yet.

The Fixer may resolve it later as Valid, Expired or Suspicious.

## Unknown

The information is not currently available.

Use sparingly.

---

# 18. Status Tokens

## Active

Default state.

The player can talk, vote, use abilities and count as a legal exit.

## Flagged

The player is under official suspicion.

Effect:

- needs one extra Supporting Document at the border
- may be affected by certain crises

## Audited

The player or one document has been officially checked.

This token is mostly a memory aid.

## Cleared

One important document has been verified.

Effect:

- helps during Final Border Check
- may protect against Suspicious failure

## Compromised

The Dossier was touched by sabotage or unreliable information.

Effect:

- future audits may be less clear
- players should treat the Dossier carefully

## Protected

The Dossier cannot be changed tonight.

Effect:

- blocks theft, forgery, fake document insertion or tampering

## Deported

The player no longer counts as a legal exit.

Effect:

- can still talk
- can still win with their team
- has one final vote for the rest of the game

## Under Review

A document or Dossier is waiting for resolution.

Effect:

- The Fixer resolves it at a later timing

# 21. Final Border Check

At the end of Day 5, The Fixer runs the Final Border Check.

This determines who legally leaves.

## Step 1: Check Heat

Use the current Heat level to determine strictness.

## Step 2: Check Active Players Only

Only Active players can count as legal exits.

Deported players do not count.

They may still win with their team.

## Step 3: Check Required Documents

At minimum, each player needs:

- Passport
- Visa Stamp
- Exit Ticket

At Heat 2+, they also need:

- 1 Supporting Document

At Heat 4+, they need:

- 2 Supporting Documents

or:

- 1 Supporting Document and 1 Cleared key document

## Step 4: Resolve Suspicious Documents

At Heat 0 to 2:

- Suspicious documents may pass.

At Heat 3:

- Suspicious documents pass only if supported.

At Heat 4+:

- Suspicious documents fail unless Cleared.

## Step 5: Count Legal Exits

For v0.1 with 9 players:

Nomads need at least **5 legal exits**.

If 5 or more players legally leave:

**Nomads can win.**

If 4 or fewer players legally leave:

**Scammers win.**

## Step 6: Check Independent Victory

After the main result, check the Independent player.

For v0.1:

Crypto Bro wins personally if 3 players bought the fake crypto-residency solution.

The Crypto Bro can win even if Nomads or Scammers also win, depending on your preferred design.

For v0.1, use:

**Independent victory is personal and separate.**

# 22. Role Reference v0.1

## Embassy Contact

Team: Nomad

Ability:

Each night, choose one player and one document type. Learn whether that document is Valid, Invalid or Unknown.

Fixer Notes:

- If blocked, answer “No result.”
- Do not explain why.
- If the document is Suspicious, answer “Suspicious” or “Unknown” depending on desired difficulty.
- For v0.1, use “Suspicious” to keep things clear.

## Remote Worker

Team: Nomad

Ability:

Once per game, protect your Dossier from sabotage tonight.

Fixer Notes:

- Protection blocks fake document insertion.
- Protection blocks document swaps.
- Protection blocks tampering.
- Protection does not automatically clear existing bad documents.

## Paranoid Planner

Team: Nomad

Ability:

Each morning, receive a hint about the day’s Crisis before the group acts.

Fixer Notes:

- Give the hint before Morning Briefing or quietly at the start of the day.
- Hint category, not exact card.
- Example: “Today’s crisis affects Exit Tickets.”

## Overstayer

Team: Nomad

Ability:

You are good, but if your Overstay Risk is publicly exposed, Heat rises by 2.

Fixer Notes:

- The Overstayer should feel suspicious but not evil.
- Do not expose them too early unless players force it.
- If exposed privately, Heat does not rise unless the rule or effect says public exposure.

## Backpacker

Team: Nomad

Ability:

You have no night ability. If Active on Day 5, your final vote counts double.

Fixer Notes:

- Simple role.
- Good for beginner players.
- Remind the player privately that survival matters.

## Local Fixer

Team: Nomad

Ability:

Once per game, repair one document or cancel one Crisis effect.

Fixer Notes:

- Can repair Expired, Suspicious or Pending documents.
- Cannot usually repair Fake documents unless you want a more forgiving game.
- If blocked, the ability fails.

## Fake Visa Agent

Team: Scammer

Ability:

Each night, add one fake or suspicious document to a player’s Dossier.

Fixer Notes:

- The target should not automatically know the document is fake.
- The Fake Visa Agent is strongest when helping good players in a way that later hurts them.
- Do not let the Fake Visa Agent create too many instant-loss documents.

## Corrupt Officer

Team: Scammer

Ability:

Each night, block one player’s ability or official check.

Fixer Notes:

- If a blocked player expects information, give “No result.”
- Do not say “You were blocked.”
- This role is meant to attack information flow.

## Crypto Bro

Team: Independent

Ability:

Win if 3 players buy your useless crypto-residency solution.

Fixer Notes:

- The Crypto Bro should create social noise.
- Their solution should sound tempting but risky.
- If accepted, usually increase Heat by 1 or make one Dossier Suspicious.

# 23. Information Rules

The Fixer should follow these rules when giving information.

## Rule 1: Answer only what was asked

If a player checks an Exit Ticket, answer about the Exit Ticket only.

Do not add:

> “Also, the player is good.”

## Rule 2: Use limited categories

Good information categories:

- Valid
- Invalid
- Suspicious
- Unknown
- No result

Avoid overly detailed answers unless the ability allows it.

## Rule 3: Good players can receive bad information

If blocked, corrupted or affected by a crisis, good players may receive:

- no result
- incomplete result
- misleading result
- delayed result

Use this carefully.

Too much false information makes the game feel random.

## Rule 4: Do not solve the game for players

The Fixer should reveal clues, not conclusions.

Say:

> “This document was added after setup.”

Do not say:

> “The Fake Visa Agent added this.”

Unless the effect specifically says so.

---

# 24. How to Run Lies and Claims

Players are allowed to lie.

Players may lie about:

- their role
- their documents
- their information
- their private conversations
- their intentions
- their team

The Fixer should not correct player lies.

If a player says something false publicly, stay neutral.

Only correct rules misunderstandings.

Example:

Player says:

> “Suspicious documents always pass.”

The Fixer may correct:

> “Rules clarification: Suspicious documents become risky as Heat rises.”

Player says:

> “I am the Embassy Contact.”

The Fixer should not confirm or deny.

# 27. Common Edge Cases

## Can Deported players talk?

Yes.

They can still talk and influence the group.

## Can Deported players vote?

In v0.1, a Deported player has one final vote for the rest of the game.

Once used, they cannot vote again.

## Can Scammers be Deported?

Yes.

A Deported Scammer no longer counts as a legal exit, but may still talk.

Their night ability usually stops unless a role says otherwise.

For v0.1:

**Deported players lose night abilities.**

## Can players show documents?

For v0.1, yes, but only during Private Chats or Public Accusation.

However, showing a document does not reveal hidden validity.

## Can players give documents to each other?

Only if a role, crisis or vote action allows it.

Do not allow free trading in v0.1 unless you want more chaos.

## Can a player know their own document is fake?

Sometimes.

By default, no.

The player sees the document face, not its hidden validity.

But a role, audit or crisis may reveal the truth.

## Does a fake document always increase Heat?

No.

It increases Heat when publicly exposed, officially audited or used in a risky crisis.

## Can the Crypto Bro win with the Nomads?

Yes.

Independent victory is personal.

For v0.1, the Crypto Bro may win personally regardless of the main team result.

# 28. Balance Guidelines v0.1

## If Nomads win too easily

Try one of these:

- Start Heat at 1.
- Add one more Suspicious document.
- Let the Fake Visa Agent create Suspicious documents that look Valid.
- Make Corrupt Officer block official checks more often.
- Require 6 legal exits instead of 5.

## If Scammers win too easily

Try one of these:

- Reduce fake documents at setup.
- Let Local Fixer repair Fake documents once.
- Give Embassy Contact clearer results.
- Keep Heat penalties lower.
- Require only 4 legal exits instead of 5.

## If players are confused

Simplify document states to:

- Valid
- Invalid
- Suspicious

Remove:

- Pending
- Tampered
- Stolen
- Wrong Name

You can add them back in v0.2.

## If the game feels too slow

Reduce to 4 days.

Use:

- Day 1: setup crisis
- Day 2: document crisis
- Day 3: social crisis
- Day 4: final border window

## If private chats are too chaotic

Use structured private chat rounds.

Example:

- Round 1: everyone must talk to someone they have not spoken to yet
- Round 2: free discussion
- Round 3: final deals

- Print document cards
- Print crisis cards
- Prepare Dossier mats
- Prepare Heat Track
- Prepare Border Clock
- Prepare status tokens
- Prepare Fixer notes
- Select 9 roles
- Prepare 9 starting Dossiers
- Choose 5 Crisis Cards
- Read “What Players Need to Know”

During the game:

- Track Heat
- Track document states
- Track blocked players
- Track fake documents
- Track Cleared and Flagged players
- Keep debate moving
- Do not overexplain
- Do not solve the game for players

After the game:

Ask players:

1. What was the most fun moment?
2. What was confusing?
3. Which role felt boring?
4. Which role felt too strong?
5. Did you understand why you won or lost?
6. Did the Dossier system feel fun or too complicated?
7. Would you play again?

# 30. Fixer Golden Rules

## 1. Keep information limited

Players should solve the puzzle.

The Fixer should not solve it for them.

## 2. Let good players look suspicious

This is part of the game.

A Nomad with bad paperwork should create tension.

## 3. Let Scammers be useful

A good Scammer should sometimes help the group.

That makes betrayal stronger.

## 4. Make Heat matter

Heat should change how players behave.

If players ignore Heat, make the border stricter.

## 5. Keep Deported players involved

Nobody should sit silently for 45 minutes.

## 6. Prefer interesting consequences over instant punishment

A fake document should create suspicion, not just kill the player’s chance immediately.

## 7. Be consistent

Use the same wording for the same type of result.

Players will build deductions from your language.

## 8. Do not fear ambiguity

Ambiguity is good.

Randomness is bad.

The difference is this:

- Ambiguity gives players clues they can debate.
- Randomness gives players outcomes they cannot understand.

# 31. v0.1 Design Goal

The goal of v0.1 is not perfect balance.

The goal is to test whether the core loop is fun:

- Players receive partial information.
- Dossiers create personal pressure.
- Scammers create confusion.
- Private chats create paranoia.
- Heat changes the stakes.
- The final border check feels tense.
- Players want to play again.

If that works, the game has a strong foundation.

Everything else can be improved later.
