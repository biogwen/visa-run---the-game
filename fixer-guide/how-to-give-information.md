# How to Give Information

The Fixer should give information in controlled categories.

## Good information

Good information is:

- Clear
- Limited
- Relevant
- Consistent with the ability

## Examples

Say:

> The document is Invalid.

Do not say:

> Hugo is evil and planted it last night.

Unless a role explicitly allows that level of detail.

## Uncertain results

Use results like:

- Valid
- Invalid
- Suspicious
- Unknown
- Cannot Confirm
