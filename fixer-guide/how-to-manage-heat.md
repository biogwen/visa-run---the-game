# How to Manage Heat

Heat should feel like rising pressure, not random punishment.

## Heat pacing

In a 5-day game, a normal Heat curve is:

- Day 1: 0 to 1
- Day 2: 1 to 2
- Day 3: 2 to 3
- Day 4: 3 to 4
- Day 5: 4 to 5

## Avoid

- Raising Heat for every small mistake
- Letting Heat stay irrelevant
- Making Heat impossible to reduce

## Good Heat use

Heat should make previous lies more dangerous at the end.
