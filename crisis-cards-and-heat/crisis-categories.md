# Crisis Categories

Crisis Cards are grouped into categories.

## Administrative Crisis

Examples: Embassy Closed, Immigration Website Down, New Visa Rule.

## Border Crisis

Examples: Random Passport Check, Exit Ticket Verification, Border Fee Increased.

## Social Crisis

Examples: Coworking Rumor, Group Chat Panic, Someone Leaked the Plan.

## Scammer Crisis

Examples: Fake Agent Arrested, Document Broker Vanished, Forged Stamp Discovered.

## Heat Crisis

Examples: Police Crackdown, Tourist Area Raid, Final Border Window.
