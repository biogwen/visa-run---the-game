# Crisis Resolution

During Crisis Reveal, The Fixer resolves the day's crisis.

## Resolution steps

1. Confirm the group decision.
2. Apply document effects.
3. Apply Heat effects.
4. Apply status tokens.
5. Announce only public information.
6. Keep hidden information hidden.

## Example

The group uses a private agent.

The Fixer says:

> The agent validates one document, but Heat increases by 1. One Dossier is now Compromised.
