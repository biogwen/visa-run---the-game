# Heat Reduction

Heat can decrease, but it should be rare.

## Common reductions

- Officially clear a key document: -1 Heat
- Deport a confirmed Scammer: -1 Heat
- Resolve a crisis cleanly: -1 Heat
- Use Local Fixer correctly: -1 Heat
- Spend a shared resource: -1 Heat

## Rule of thumb

Heat should rise more easily than it falls.
