# Heat Increase Triggers

Heat increases when the group attracts attention.

## Common triggers

- Fake document exposed: +1 Heat
- Overstay Risk revealed: +2 Heat
- Illegal solution used: +1 or +2 Heat
- Crisis failed: +1 Heat
- Full Dossier Audit reveals serious issue: +1 Heat
- Stolen document exposed: +2 Heat
- Group fails to act during a crisis: +1 Heat

## Fixer advice

Do not raise Heat for every small mistake. Heat should feel meaningful.
