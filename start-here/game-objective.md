# Game Objective

The objective depends on your team.

## Nomads

Nomads win if enough players can legally leave before the border closes.

They usually need to:

- Keep Heat under control
- Detect Scammer patterns
- Repair or validate key documents
- Avoid wasting audits on good players with minor problems
- Reach the Final Border Check with enough valid Dossiers

## Scammers

Scammers win if they prevent enough players from leaving.

They usually do this by:

- Planting fake documents
- Blocking useful abilities
- Increasing Heat
- Making good players look guilty
- Pushing bad advice
- Forcing audits that expose good players' complications

## Independents

Independents have personal win conditions. They may help or harm the group depending on their objective.

An Independent can win even if the Nomads or Scammers win the main game, unless their role says otherwise.
