# Player Count and Duration

Visa Run is designed for 7 to 15 players.

## Recommended player counts

| Players | Recommended Experience |
|---:|---|
| 7 | Short beginner game |
| 8 to 9 | Best first prototype size |
| 10 to 12 | Standard social deduction size |
| 13 to 15 | Advanced, chaotic and high social pressure |

## Recommended duration

| Experience | Duration |
|---|---:|
| First game | 75 to 90 minutes |
| Experienced group | 45 to 75 minutes |
| High chaos script | 75 to 120 minutes |

## Fixer advice

For a first game, use 9 players if possible. It creates enough social complexity without overwhelming The Fixer.
