# What is Visa Run?

**Visa Run: The Border Is Closing** is a hidden-role social deduction game about digital nomads trying to leave a country before the border closes.

Most players are **Nomads** trying to escape legally. Some players are **Scammers** trying to sabotage the group. Some players are **Independents** with personal objectives.

The core question is not only:

> Who is evil?

The better question is:

> Who can actually leave, who is making Dossiers harder to trust, and whose advice will ruin the group?

The updated beginner game uses **Full Dossier Checks** instead of document-by-document audits.
