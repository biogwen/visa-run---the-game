# What is Visa Run?

**Visa Run: The Border Is Closing** is a hidden-role social deduction game for 7 to 15 players.

Players are digital nomads trapped in a country where the border will close soon. To escape, they need valid documents. Some players are Scammers who sabotage the group. Some are Independents with selfish objectives.

One player is **The Fixer**, the Game Master. The Fixer runs the night phase, manages hidden information, reveals crises, tracks Heat and resolves the Final Border Check.

## What makes it different

Visa Run is not a simple good team versus evil team game. Many good players have paperwork problems. They may lie, hide information or avoid audits because their Dossier is fragile.

Scammers do not only need to hide. They need to make the Nomads distrust each other, increase Heat, plant fake documents and push bad decisions.

## The core fantasy

You are not hunting werewolves. You are trying to survive bureaucracy, scams, bad advice and border pressure.

The table should feel like a chaotic digital nomad group chat where everyone has a plan, nobody has the full context, and one person is definitely lying about knowing someone at immigration.
