# Traveller and Guest Roles

Traveller and Guest roles are optional.

Use them when:

- A player joins late
- A player must leave early
- The group wants a lighter role
- Someone is new to social deduction games

## Example Guest roles

### Hostel Volunteer

You may vote normally, but you do not count as a legal exit.

### Late Arrival

You start without a full Dossier. The group may help you complete it.

### Tourist Friend

Once per game, you may ask The Fixer whether a public statement is rules-legal.
