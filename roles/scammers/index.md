# Scammer Roles

## Fake Visa Agent

Each night, add one **Unofficial Document** to a player's Dossier.

It starts Pending.

If used before being Cleared, it becomes Fake.

## Corrupt Officer

Each night, weaken one player's next Official Check or role ability.

Examples:

- Green becomes Yellow
- Yellow becomes Orange
- Orange becomes Red
- role check gives Unknown

## Passport Thief

Steals or swaps documents. More advanced, often used as a bluff in beginner scripts.
