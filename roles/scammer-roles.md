# Scammer Roles

## Fake Visa Agent

**Ability:** Each night, choose one player. You may add one Fake or Suspicious document to their Dossier.

**Purpose:** Makes good players defend bad documents.

## Corrupt Officer

**Ability:** Each night, choose one player. Their ability is blocked tonight.

**Purpose:** Stops key information and repairs.

## Passport Thief

**Ability:** Twice per game, swap one document between two players.

**Purpose:** Creates false accusations.

## Bad Advisor

**Ability:** Once per day, choose one player. The next information they receive may be incomplete or misleading.

**Purpose:** Corrupts the information economy.

## SIM Card Scammer

**Ability:** Once per game, listen to part of a private conversation, then publicly report one sentence truthfully or falsely.

**Purpose:** Makes private chats dangerous.

## Immigration Trap

**Ability:** If the group sends you to the embassy or trusts you as an official contact, Heat increases by 2.

**Purpose:** Punishes lazy trust.
