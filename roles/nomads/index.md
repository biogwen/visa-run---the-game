# Nomad Roles

## Embassy Contact

Checks one player and one document type.

## Remote Worker

Protects their Dossier once.

## Paranoid Planner

Receives Crisis hints.

## Overstayer

Good, but public exposure of Overstay Risk causes Heat +2.

## Backpacker

No night ability. Final vote counts double if Active on Day 5.

## Local Fixer

Repairs one document or cancels one Crisis effect.

## Border Runner

Moves one Supporting Document.

## Travel Agent

Improves one Exit Ticket.

## Document Clerk

Reads Green, Yellow, Orange or Red once.

## Embassy Intern

Checks whether an Embassy result was affected by a Scammer.

## Insurance Broker

Prevents one Flagged result.
