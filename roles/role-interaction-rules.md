# Role Interaction Rules

This page gives general rulings for role interactions.

## Blocking beats checking

If a player is blocked, their ability produces no result unless the role says otherwise.

## Protection beats tampering

If a Dossier is Protected, it cannot be changed by theft, forgery, tampering or forced swaps.

## Repair beats Expired, not Fake

A standard repair can fix Expired, Suspicious or Pending documents. It does not automatically fix Fake documents unless the role says so.

## Corrupted information

If information is corrupted, The Fixer should give misleading but plausible information. Do not make it absurd.
