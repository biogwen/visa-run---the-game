# Shared Victory

Shared Victory refers to the main team result.

## Nomad shared victory

Nomads win together if enough Active players legally exit.

A Nomad who fails personally at the border may still win if the Nomad team condition is met, unless their role says otherwise.

## Scammer shared victory

Scammers win together if they stop enough exits.

A Scammer does not need to personally pass the border unless their role says so.

## Why this matters

The game is social. Players should care about the group outcome, not only their own file.
