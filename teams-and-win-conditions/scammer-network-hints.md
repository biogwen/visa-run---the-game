# Scammer Network Hints

During Night 0, each Scammer receives a **Network Hint**.

A Network Hint is a list of 3 names.

In that list:

- at least 1 player is a Scammer
- there may be 1 Independent
- the rest may be Nomads

Example:

> Your network may include: Hugo, Maya, Leo.

Scammers have clues, but not certainty.
