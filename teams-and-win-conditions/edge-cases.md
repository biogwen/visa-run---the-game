# Edge Cases

This page covers common win condition edge cases.

## A Scammer legally exits

A Scammer may legally exit, but this does not help the Nomad victory threshold unless the script says all legal exits count.

Recommended prototype rule: all legal exits count for the border total, but Scammers still win only if the Nomad threshold is not met.

## A Deported Nomad would have had valid documents

They do not count as a legal exit. Deportation removes them from official processing.

## An Independent completes their objective but is Deported

Check the role text. If it does not require being Active, they may still win.

## Heat reaches lockdown

At Heat 6, Scammers usually win unless a specific rescue effect is active.
