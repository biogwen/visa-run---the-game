# Personal Victory

Personal Victory belongs mostly to Independents, but some scripts may give personal objectives to other roles.

## Resolving personal victory

Resolve personal objectives after the main team outcome, unless a role says it resolves immediately.

## Examples

- Crypto Bro: wins if 3 players bought the fake solution.
- Chill Guy: wins if the game reaches Day 5 and they are not Deported.
- Fake Rich Nomad: wins if they leave without valid Proof of Funds.

## Important rule

A personal victory can coexist with a Nomad or Scammer team victory if the role allows it.
